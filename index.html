Welcome to CIE-Sharp

CIE-Sharp is a web-based microgame learning material designed to supplement ICT students' motivation in learning the C# programming language through interactive lessons and game-based activities.

Meet the CIE-Sharp Research Team

CIE-Sharp was developed through the collaborative work of BSIE-ICT students of the College of Industrial Education, Technological University of the Philippines – Manila. The research team worked together in planning, designing, developing, and evaluating the web-based microgame learning material for C# programming.
