Welcome to CIE-Sharp

CIE-Sharp is a web-based microgame learning material designed to supplement ICT students' motivation in learning the C# programming language through interactive lessons and game-based activities.

About CIE-Sharp

CIE-Sharp provides an interactive learning environment where students can study fundamental C# programming concepts through short lessons, microgames, and formative assessments.

Features

Microgames

Learn C# through fun and interactive challenges.

Quizzes

Practice syntax, variables, loops, and conditions.

Progress Tracking

Monitor your learning journey and achievements.

Target Users

Developed for 2nd Year ICT students of the College of Industrial Education, Technological University of the Philippines - Manila.
