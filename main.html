Welcome to CIE-Sharp

A Web-Based Microgame Learning Material designed to supplement ICT students' motivation in learning the C# Programming Language.

Start Learning

Why CIE-Sharp?

Many ICT students struggle with basic C# programming concepts. CIE-Sharp provides interactive learning experiences through microgames, quizzes, and instant feedback.

Features

Microgames

Learn C# through fun and interactive challenges.

Quizzes

Practice syntax, variables, loops, and conditions.

Progress Tracking

Monitor your learning journey and achievements.

Target Users

Developed for 2nd Year ICT students of the College of Industrial Education, Technological University of the Philippines - Manila.
